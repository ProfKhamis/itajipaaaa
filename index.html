Digit Even/Odd Strategy

Pattern Over/Under

Watches the latest ticks. If the Over 2 trigger digits (2,0 by default; any number of digits works) land back-to-back (in the order typed or reversed), it fires an Over 2 contract. If the Under 7 trigger digits (7,9 by default) land back-to-back (in the order typed or reversed), it fires an Under 7 contract. Both triggers can be changed below at any time. One button drives both directions automatically — no manual side selection needed. Each pattern fires a burst of trades back-to-back: the Over/Under, then Differs runs on that same barrier digit (2 by default, set below), each sent the instant the previous trade settles and with the stake compounded (previous stake + its profit, never below your base stake). If any trade loses, the burst stops and an Even/Odd martingale recovery takes over: it waits for a strike (the chosen number of even or odd digits in a row) and trades the opposite, with the stake multiplied. A win ends it; at most 2 recovery trades are made, then the normal pattern watching resumes. Otherwise it stops after the burst and waits for the next pattern. Max Trades counts every trade (with the default 2 Differs runs, that is 3 per pattern).

Sentinel — Flat-Stake Differs with an Edge Audit

The strategy I would run myself. Synthetic indices are random, so no pattern can beat them; every contract carries a small built-in cost, and Digit Differs is the cheapest one (about 90% win chance, around 1.4% expected cost per dollar staked, versus roughly 2.5% on Rise/Fall). So Sentinel is about damage control, not prediction: it trades Differs on the digit that just printed (bought the instant it prints, so it enters on the very next tick), with a flat stake — no martingale, no compounding, a minimum gap between trades, an automatic pause after a losing streak, and hard run limits (stop loss, take profit, max trades). The Edge Audit below compares your real win rate with the break-even win rate implied by the actual payouts, with a 95% confidence range, so the numbers (not hope) tell you whether to keep going. Expect it to hover slightly below break-even; its job is to keep losses small and slow.

Under Edge — Under-Only, Cheapest Contract First

Trades only Digit Under.     Magic Recovery

    A one-to-one port of the Deriv Bot Builder strategy "Magic Recovery" (Digits Over/Under, 1 tick). Every trade it picks a random number from 0 to 9, reads your current Loss counter, and uses the two rule ladders below to choose Over or Under and the prediction digit. A win resets the stake to your Win Stake and the Loss counter to 0. A loss adds 1 to the Loss counter and sets the next stake to the absolute loss multiplied by the recovery multiplier (2.2 in the original). It stops at your Expected Profit, your Stop Loss, or after the set number of runs. Designed for Volatility 10 (1s) Index, but it uses whatever market you have streaming.
