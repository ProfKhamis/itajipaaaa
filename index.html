Rise/Fall Signal

Plots real OHLC candles for the market selected above and reads them the way a price-action trader does: swing structure (bullish = higher highs & higher lows, bearish = the reverse), support and resistance built from repeated swing points, candlestick patterns, the candle that is forming right now, and tick momentum. It only signals when those readings agree; otherwise it says stand aside. The scorecard below grades every signal against what price actually did. This is analysis, not a guarantee — Volatility Indices are generated by Deriv's own random number engine, so chart reading has no proven edge on them. Watch the scorecard on your market before staking real money, and never risk more than you can afford to lose.
